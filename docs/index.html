// CONTRACT

How a run goes

01

Wake the relic

Every hold hides a Chain-Scale. Touch it and the guild's clock starts: waves spawn, the pumps fail, and the water begins to climb.

02

Fight in the flood

The water is not scenery. It slows you, drags loose scrap toward the drain, and rises with every wave you clear. Hold the high ground or keep moving.

03

Salvage and refit

Break crates and enemies for scrap, then spend it at the terminal: unlock a new weapon, install its module, or harden the suit. Everything you buy carries to the next hold.

ExtractReach the pad before the four-minute window closes. Then go deeper.

// FIELD WORKSHOP

Three more ways to survive the flood

The Pulse Blaster and Runic Charge come with the suit. Scrap buys the rest, one at a time, and each takes an upgrade module.

Arc Welder

Hold to chain lightning

The arc hits the nearest enemy in front of you and jumps to three more. Anything standing in the flood takes more and conducts it further.

Tractor Beam

Pull, then slam

Haul loose scrap across the hold, drag drones into your line of fire, and slow the heavies. Hold it a second and let go to slam the tip.

Mine Layer

Make them walk into it

Drop proximity mines where the pack will come through. Three stay live. The blast breaks crates and walls, and it does not care whose boots are nearby.

Enemies that hunt like a pack

Wake Drones

They stage, then rush

Drones wait out of sight until the pack is assembled, then come in from several sides at once.

Tidecasters

They lead their shots

Casters find a line of sight, fire volleys that cover your strafe, then relocate before you can answer.

Iron Wardens

They charge through cover

Armored front, telegraphed charge, slow to turn. Flank them or bring the Runic Charge.

// FIELD KIT

Download and play

Download for Windows

Latest build · Windows 10/11 64-bit · zip

  1. Extract the zip somewhere you own, such as Documents or Desktop. Not Program Files: the game updates itself in place.
  2. Run RansWake/RansWake.exe. The build is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it protected your PC. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Stay current. New builds download in the background while you play, verified against their published SHA-256. When one is ready the game asks: Install and restart or Later. A failed install rolls back. (Builds 66 to 69: pause once and choose Update.)
